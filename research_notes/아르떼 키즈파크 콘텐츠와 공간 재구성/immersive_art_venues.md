# Immersive media-art and art-themed family venues as comparables for Arte Kids Park USA

Research date: 2026-11-05. Scope: teamLab Future Park/Borderless, Meow Wolf, Crayola Experience, Museum of Ice Cream, Color Factory, Superblue, Peppa Pig Theme Park, and d'strict's own Arte Kids Park (Korea). Research was capped at about 17 tool calls, so coverage is uneven. ARTECHOUSE, Candytopia, Sesame Place, Hello Kitty indoor parks, teamLab Planets kids content and "Little Big Things" could not be verified (see Gaps).

## Q1. teamLab Future Park / teamLab kids areas: installations, and the balance between body play and drawing ("Sketch")

### Takeaway
teamLab's kids format ("Learn & Play! teamLab Future Park") mixes three content types: drawing that gets scanned and projected (Sketch Aquarium, Sketch Town, Graffiti Nature), body-movement projection play (Hopscotch for Geniuses, Sliding through the Fruit Field, Light Ball Orchestra), and block or table play (Connecting! Block Town, A Table where Little People Live). At Borderless Azabudai the physical "Athletics Forest" is a separate zone from the co-creation "Future Park" zone. teamLab splits body play and making into different spaces rather than blending them. Line-ups are modular and change by venue and year.

### Cited Findings
- The official Future Park list of play installations includes Sketch Aquarium, Light Ball Orchestra, Hopscotch for Geniuses, Sketch Town, Sliding through the Fruit Field, Graffiti Nature, Sketch Animals, Sketch Flight, Connecting! Block Town, A Table where Little People Live, and Waterfall Droplets. — [teamLab Future Park play installations](https://futurepark.teamlab.art/en/playinstallations) (I fetched the page but its body text did not render. The names come from the search index snippet and press coverage below.)
- How the installations work:
  - Sketch Aquarium: a child draws a sea creature, and the drawing is scanned and projected into a virtual aquarium.
  - Light Ball Orchestra: large light balls make sounds when tapped or bounced, and the music grows fuller as more people join.
  - Hopscotch for Geniuses: stepping on shapes in sequence produces birds, fish or butterflies.
  - Sketch Town: drawn vehicles and buildings fill a shared town.
  - Sources: [ScreenHub review of Powerhouse Sydney edition](https://www.screenhub.com.au/news/reviews/learn-and-play-teamlab-future-park-254860-1424615/); [ArtsHub](https://www.artshub.com.au/?p=2358051); [Ellaslist](https://ellaslist.com.au/articles/sydney-s-getting-a-light-up-playground)
- The format is modular. The Sydney Powerhouse edition had 8 installations, and the Ibaraki (Yuki) kids park had 4 sections. — [Time Out Tokyo](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/news/teamlab-has-a-new-interactive-art-park-for-kids-in-ibaraki-adults-are-welcome-too-011122); [ScreenHub](https://www.screenhub.com.au/news/reviews/learn-and-play-teamlab-future-park-254860-1424615/)
- Future Park installations also run as long-term placements in Japanese shopping malls (venue pages for LaLaport Fujimi, Hirakata, Itami/Itemehime and others). — [Future Park LaLaport Fujimi](https://futurepark.teamlab.art/fr/places/lalaportfujimi/); [Hirakata](https://futurepark.teamlab.art/fr/places/hirakata)
- At teamLab Borderless (Azabudai Hills, Tokyo), the Athletics Forest is a "creative physical space" for running, jumping, sliding and climbing, including a bouncy galaxy-like structure and giant inflatable balls. A separate Future Park zone is a "collaborative space for co-creation" where the artwork evolves as people create together. — [Japan Nakama](https://www.japannakama.co.uk/travel/guides-travel/teamlab-locations-japan/); [Japan City Tour](https://japancitytour.com/teamlab/); [Business Wire teamLab release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241030183851/en)
- Visitors walk the Athletics Forest and Future Park in shoes, unlike the barefoot Water areas. — [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241030183851/en)
- Superblue Miami (which features teamLab) operated into 2026: a ticketing listing carries reviews dated January 2026. One guide says children aged about 5–12 respond well, but warns that toddlers may find the dark, loud rooms unsettling. A travel guide quotes child tickets (ages 3–12) at $14 and under-3s free. This price is unverified and may be out of date. — [Fever listing](https://www.feverup.com/m/268290); [Travelated guide](https://www.travelated.com/usa/florida/miami/superblue-miami-everything-you-need-to-know-before-you-visit?locale=en); [Superblue about](https://superblue.com/about)

### Inferences
- Of the 11 Future Park titles found, roughly 5 are Sketch/drawing titles and roughly 4 are body-movement titles; the rest are table/block or ambient pieces. At Borderless the physical Athletics Forest probably takes more floor area than the drawing zone, but I could not verify floor areas.
- Two teamLab patterns fit AKP USA's "repeat play / creation / exploration" axes without collection mechanics:
  - The drawing shows up in a shared world, so the reward is seeing your own creation, not collecting anything.
  - Each piece stands alone, so a visit is many short sessions.
- Many Future Park pieces run as short-loop interactions. For example, a Sketch drawing appears within a minute or two. This fits 1–3 minute sessions, but I found no published dwell-time figures.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm a named "Athletics Forest" inside the Future Park installation catalogue. It is a Borderless zone name.
- I found no published floor areas for Athletics Forest or Future Park zones, no dwell times, and no ticket prices.
- I did not verify teamLab Planets kids content, US children's-museum installations, or teamLab Borderless Jeddah/Hamburg details.

## Q2. Meow Wolf: family/kid appeal, zoning, open-ended exploration, repeat visits and annual passes

### Takeaway
Meow Wolf is not a kids' venue. It is an all-ages exploration environment made of artist-built rooms, with typical visits of 2–3+ hours and no published minimum age. Its main repeat-visit mechanism is the Annual Portal Pass, either for one location or all locations ("Supremium").

### Cited Findings
- Children 3 and under are free, ages 4–12 pay a child price, and anyone under 16 must be with an adult. — [Meow Wolf FAQ: ages](https://faq.meowwolf.com/for-what-ages-is-meow-wolf-appropriate-is-meow-wolf-scary); [Kids visit free FAQ](https://faq.meowwolf.com/kids-visit-free)
- The FAQ warns that some guests may feel overstimulated and suggests earplugs or headphones for children. — [Meow Wolf FAQ](https://faq.meowwolf.com/for-what-ages-is-meow-wolf-appropriate-is-meow-wolf-scary)
- Parent reviewers warn that young children (under about 8) can get lost and that some areas could scare younger kids. — [Macaroni KID Santa Fe](https://rosevilleca.macaronikid.com/articles/5ac2bedf283a9714c0bcdc23/meow-wolf-santa-fe); [Macaroni KID Denver](https://lincoln.macaronikid.com/articles/64becb87d3e4eb58e0e7b6c3/road-trip-review-meow-wolf-in-denver-co)
- Dwell time comes from third-party sources only: about 2–3 hours recommended for Santa Fe, and 3–4 hours reported by a Denver visitor. — [Travel Buddies](https://travel-buddies.com/meow-wolfs-house-of-eternal-return/); [Erika's Travel Ventures](https://www.erikastravelventures.com/tips-for-visiting-meow-wolf-in-denver)
- Venue sizes:
  - Santa Fe is about 20K sq ft, Las Vegas about 52K sq ft, and Denver about 90K sq ft. — [Austin Chronicle](https://www.austinchronicle.com/columns/2023-03-24/day-trips-meow-wolf-grapevine-and-houston/print)
  - Grapevine is a 40,340 sq ft former Bed Bath & Beyond building, with about 29,000 sq ft of exhibit space split into about 30 artist rooms. — [Dallas CultureMap](https://dallas.culturemap.com/news/arts/05-11-22-meow-wolf-immersive-arts-dfw-grapevine-mills); [Dallas Innovates](https://dallasinnovates.com/?p=230614)
  - Source conflict on Grapevine: the Austin Chronicle gives 24K sq ft.
- Annual Portal Pass:
  - The basic pass gives one year of entry at a single location, and you can hold a pass for only one exhibition at a time. — [Meow Wolf FAQ](https://faq.meowwolf.com/annual-passes-to-more-than-one-exhibition); [Press release](https://meowwolf.com/press-releases/meow-wolf-launches-new-annual-portal-pass)
  - The "Supremium" tier gives unlimited visits to all locations. — [Meow Wolf Portal Pass page](https://meowwolf.com/portal-pass)
  - Holders get 10% off retail, food and extra tickets, or 20% for Supremium. Some dates are blacked out. — [Discounts FAQ](https://faq.meowwolf.com/discounts-annual-portal-pass)
  - The pass launched in Denver in Nov 2023 at $84 for adults and $74 for children. These are 2023 prices. — [Denverite](https://denverite.com/2023/11/21/meow-wolf-launches-yearly-pass-denver-location-convergence-station/)
- The Las Vegas (Omega Mart) annual pass was introduced in 2024. — [Las Vegas Magazine](https://lasvegasmagazine.com/entertainment/2024/feb/06/omega-mart-portal-pass-area15-las-vegas-attraction/)
- Meow Wolf sites are described as certified autism centers. — [Fox 4](https://www.fox4news.com/texas-to-do-list/meow-wolf-grapevine-texas-to-do-list)
- The pass also covers 21+ night events ("Adulti-Verse/Night Shift"), which separates adult programming from daytime family visits. — [Meow Wolf FAQ](https://faq.meowwolf.com/annual-pass-for-concerts-special-events)

### Inferences
- Meow Wolf shows what open-ended exploration looks like: hidden doors, a non-linear layout and story clues. Its long dwell time conflicts with AKP's 1–3 minute session model. What AKP can borrow is the discovery layer, such as hidden passages and surprise reveals, not the narrative depth.
- The annual pass, with retail and F&B discounts, is a proven US repeat-visit tool that needs no collection mechanic.

### Gaps
- I found no official age recommendation and no official dwell time.
- I found no kid-specific zones at any Meow Wolf, and no current 2026 pass prices.
- I did not verify Houston details.

## Q3. Crayola Experience, Museum of Ice Cream, Color Factory, Superblue, ARTECHOUSE, Candytopia, IP parks (Peppa Pig etc.)

### Takeaway
Crayola Experience is the closest US comparable by content type. Its 20–25 short attractions mix four things:
- making stations
- digital "draw, scan, see it come alive" pieces
- motion-interactive projection floors and walls
- physical play: a climber, a toddler area and a water table

It also offers parties, seasonal events and a low-price annual pass. Two dates matter: Crayola Plano closed on Feb 6, 2026, and only Easton, Orlando and Mall of America remain listed. Museum of Ice Cream and Color Factory are photo-room venues built around one signature physical feature (the sprinkle pool, the ball pit). They are all-ages, not kid-specific.

### Cited Findings
**Crayola Experience**
- Plano closed permanently on Feb 6, 2026. Annual pass holders were redirected to the remaining locations: Easton PA (flagship), Orlando (Florida Mall) and Bloomington MN (Mall of America). Chandler AZ is not listed, which suggests it has also closed, but I did not verify this. — [Crayola Experience Plano page](https://www.crayolaexperience.com/plano/things-to-do)
- Plano was 60,000 sq ft with 22 attractions at The Shops at Willow Bend mall. A 2024 source lists admission at $24.99 online and $27.99 at the door, with an annual pass at $37.99. — [Visit Plano](https://visitplano.com/attractions/crayola-experience)
- Easton has 25 attractions across 4 floors and 65,000 sq ft. — [Crayola Experience Easton things to do](https://www.crayolaexperience.com/easton/things-to-do)
- Easton's attractions by content type (same source):
  - **Making/craft:** Activity Studio, Drip Art (melted-wax spin art), Melt & Mold, Paint Palette (watercolor), Puzzle It!, Texture CRAYze, Wrap It Up (custom crayon label), Colossal Caddy (giant coloring station), Scribble Square (color on walls), Scribble Scrubbies (color and wash a toy pet).
  - **Digital creation (draw/scan/animate):** Model Magic Movie Studios (sculpt a creature that stars in an animated episode), Cartoon Creator (a robot sketches you, then you color), Be a Star (star in a coloring page), Silly Selfies (animation), You Design (design and scan an outfit for a virtual runway), Create & Play (app).
  - **Body-interactive projection:** Rainbow Rain (catch and bounce digital melted wax with your movement), Stomp and Play (interactive floor games).
  - **Physical play:** Color Playground (2-story climber with a melted-wax tower and paint bridge), Toddler Town (mini-climber and pegboard), Water Works (85-ft water table).
  - **Shows/meet:** Crayon Factory Show, Chaos and Creativity Show, Character Appearances, Big Blue photo op (a 1,500-lb crayon).
- Easton's repeat-visit and programming hooks: an annual pass ("12 months of family fun"), Toddler Tuesdays, Sensory Sundays, Crayola After Dark, Colorful Christmas and birthday parties. — [Crayola Easton](https://www.crayolaexperience.com/easton/things-to-do); [Crayola Plano Colorful Christmas](https://www.crayolaexperience.com/plano/things-to-do/colorful-christmas)
- Silly Selfies and Kaleidoscope were added as new attractions in Texas. This shows attractions get refreshed. — [Visit Plano](https://www.visitplano.com/the-colorful-crayola-experience-opens-in-plano)

**Museum of Ice Cream**
- The signature sprinkle pool is filled with non-edible antimicrobial plastic sprinkles. A 2018 San Francisco visitor reported only 3 minutes allowed in the pool. — [Museum of Ice Cream](https://www.museumoficecream.com/?p=740); [Fox 7 Austin](https://fox7austin.com/news/inside-the-sprinkle-pool-at-the-museum-of-ice-cream-in-san-francisco)
- Chicago: a general admission listing describes 14 interactive installations, and a ticket listing mentions unlimited treats plus themed crafts. — [Virgin Experience Gifts Chicago](https://www.virgingifts.com/product/illinois/chicago/museum-of-ice-cream-general-admission-2)

**Color Factory**
- Reviewers mostly suggest ages 4+. The ball pit is the most-praised feature, and a Chicago listing says several rooms are not interesting for preschoolers. Strollers are not allowed in the exhibits. One guide estimates about 90 minutes for a visit. — [New York Family](https://newyorkfamily.com/color-factory-with-kids-new-yorks-brightest-attraction-reviewed); [Loop Chicago](https://loopchicago.com/es/listing/color-factory/); [Houstonia](https://www.houstoniamag.com/arts-and-culture/color-factory-houston-tickets-prices-info)

**Peppa Pig Theme Park (Merlin)**
- The Peppa Pig Theme Park in North Richland Hills (Dallas–Fort Worth) is aimed at children up to about age 6. It combines rides, themed playgrounds, character shows and an indoor cinema, and has an indoor pedal-bike and tricycle track. It was named one of USA Today's top 10 best new attractions (Jan 2026). — [BSC Kids](https://www.bsckids.com/2026/01/peppa-pig-theme-park-dallas-fort-worth-named-one-of-usa-todays-top-10-best-new-attractions); [Planet Attractions](https://www.planetattractions.com/news/Texass-Peppa-Pig-Theme-Park-sets-opening-date-for-2025/3690)
- There is also a Florida park next to LEGOLAND Florida. Merlin also runs "Peppa Pig World of Play" indoor play centers for preschoolers. — [Attractions Management](https://www.attractionsmanagement.com/attractions-news/Further-details-revealed-for-Orlandos-Peppa-Pig-theme-park/348367); [License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/location-based-entertainment/merlin-entertainments-hasbro-reveal-peppa-pig-attractions)

### Inferences
- Crayola's content beyond drawing classes is a useful checklist for AKP USA:
  - draw/sculpt, then scan and animate (a digital "make it come alive" pipeline)
  - motion-reactive floors and walls
  - a multi-level climber with art theming
  - a water table
  - a dedicated toddler zone
  - personalization stations (custom label, star in your page)
  - a live demonstration show
  - character meets
  - sensory-friendly sessions and themed nights or holidays
- Crayola's take-home items (custom crayon, toy pet) are keepsakes, not collection sets, which shows a non-collection reward model. Both Crayola's and Museum of Ice Cream's attractions are short, roughly a few minutes each, which supports AKP's 1–3 minute design. This rests on description only; I found no official dwell figures.
- Plano closed despite a dense family market, and Chandler seems to have closed too. Together these suggest the mall-based branded-art model in the US carries real risk. I found no published reason for the closures.

### Gaps
- I could not verify ARTECHOUSE kids programming, Candytopia's status, Sesame Place, Hello Kitty indoor parks, or "Little Big Things".
- I did not check Color Factory or Museum of Ice Cream membership options or 2026 status.
- I did not verify the Crayola Chandler closure or current Orlando and Mall of America line-ups.
- I found no official dwell times for any of these venues.

## Q4. d'strict's own Arte Kids Park (Korea): existing content

### Takeaway
Arte Kids Park Byeollae (Namyangju, opened July 2026) is about 600 pyeong (about 2,000 m²) with 4 zones and 15+ contents. It blends physical play built around a 22 m media façade with art and sensory making, floor interaction, and a ceiling-projection rest area for parents. It uses a tagging-band mission system with about 60 tag points. AKP USA has explicitly dropped this system (no RFID, no collection).

### Cited Findings
- Operator d'strict. Opened in July 2026 at Byeollae Xi The Star (별내자이더스타), Namyangju. Theme is "Joyful Nature." About 600 pyeong. Four zones: Play, Experience, Dynamic and Exhibition, with more than 15 play and experience contents. — [Unicorn Factory, 2026-07-13](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2026071314330464087)
- Contents by zone (same source):
  - **Play Zone** (in front of a 22 m-wide media façade): Ball Swing, Roof Climbing, Challenger Gym, Slide Canvas.
  - **Experience Zone:** Live Sketchbook (a child's drawing comes alive on the wall), Color Ground, Sand Beach (real sand).
  - **Dynamic Zone:** Garden Stepping (movement-reactive floor), Sky Riding (pedal or bike ride).
  - **Exhibition Zone "Oasis":** a rest area for guardians under ceiling-wide media art.
- About 60 tag points let visitors complete missions and earn points with a tagging band. The target age covers preschool and elementary, which is wider than Jeju. Opening promotion: 10% off child tickets, or up to 20% when bundled with the 째깍섬 kids-class venue in the same building. — [Unicorn Factory](https://www.unicornfactory.co.kr/article/2026071314330464087)
- Jeju (the first site, opened 2024) has a 16-activity play zone and three experiential media spaces, themed "Joyful Nature." Its media art is swapped by season; for example, autumn 2024 was Halloween-themed with pumpkin props. — [HEYPOP](https://heypop.kr/n/108664/) (from the search summary; I did not fetch the full article)

### Inferences
- The Korean model already pairs body play (climbing, swinging, sliding, cycling) with a drawing-to-projection piece (Live Sketchbook, like teamLab's Sketch) and sensory material play (sand). For the US, the tag-mission layer would have to be replaced by non-collection re-play hooks, such as:
  - seasonal media swaps, as Jeju already does
  - annual passes, as Meow Wolf and Crayola offer
  - sensory, toddler or themed sessions, as Crayola runs
- The parent rest zone with ceiling media ("Oasis") is a distinctive element. None of the US comparables documented here have an equivalent.

### Gaps
- I found no Byeollae ticket prices, hours, dwell time or per-zone areas.
- I did not fetch the full Jeju content list or Jeju's size.
- I did not research ARTE MUSEUM's content in this pass.
